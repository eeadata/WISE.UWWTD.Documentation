# Descriptive dataset

The descriptive dataset holds the national implementation programme itself: the national summary
and self-assessment, the derogations requested, the planned measures with their cost and
financing, and the state of the plants and agglomerations they concern.

Each page describes one table: what it is for, when it is required, and every field with its
notation, label, definition, data type, requirement status and any condition or codelist.
Requirement statuses are explained in {ref}`art23-requirement-status`.

Documents referred to from these tables are reported in the {ref}`art23-documents-dataset`.
