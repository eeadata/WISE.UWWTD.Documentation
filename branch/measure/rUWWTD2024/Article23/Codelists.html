Codelists#

Warning

Draft All codelists on this page are proposals, and several are under review (Open issues and proposed changes).

Every codelist below is named as a WISE enumeration type, given under the heading, and gives for each value the notation to report, a short label and a definition, following Notation, label and definition.

Each codelist field takes one value unless its type is marked [0..n].

Measure type#

Type: UWWTDMeasureType

Used by the uwwtdMeasureType field of the Measure table. One value per measure. The codelist brings together the former treatment plant and agglomeration measure codelists; the renewal value, which both had, is split into one value for collecting systems and one for treatment plants. The notes give the article and the object type: a measure listing plants or agglomerations in MeasureObject must list objects of that type.

The name is prefixed with UWWTD because the Water Framework Directive codelist MeasureType classifies measures differently, by their nature (legislative, technical, economic and so on).

Correspondence with the Water Framework Directive#

Measures for treatment plants and collecting systems are intended to be reported once, in this dataflow, and not again in the programme of measures under the Water Framework Directive (OI-25 Reporting measures once). So that they can still be used in assessments under that Directive, each measure type corresponds to a WFD key type of measure (mainKeyTypeOfMeasure) and main pressure (mainPressureType), derived centrally and not reported.
