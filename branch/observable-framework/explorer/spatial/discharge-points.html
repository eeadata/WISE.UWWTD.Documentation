Discharge points provisional

These are the that will be prefilled in the first delivery of the spatial points dataflow, from the reporting of reference year under Directive 91/271/EEC. That reporting is provisional and under Member State review, and the prefill itself is still being developed: names, locations, codes and flags may change. Not an EEA publication.

Every row starts as noChange: in the delivery, the Member State confirms it, corrects it, or retires or replaces it. Select a point on the map or a row in the table to see the values that will be prefilled and the checks to look at. The fields are defined in the (draft).
