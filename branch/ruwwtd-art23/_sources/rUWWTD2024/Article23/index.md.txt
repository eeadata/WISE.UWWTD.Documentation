# Article 23 - National implementation programmes

:::{warning} Draft after consultation comments
This section describes the proposed reporting format from the draft reporting guidance.
Nothing here is final: table names, field names, data types, codelists and conditions may
still change.
:::

Article 23 of the revised Urban Wastewater Treatment Directive
{footcite:p}`urbanWastewaterTreatmentDirectiveRecast` requires each Member State to establish a
national implementation programme (NIP) by 1 January 2028 and to submit it to the Commission,
unless it demonstrates compliance with Articles 3 to 8. This section explains what Reporters
are asked to provide and how to fill in each table.
