Reporting timing and reference dates#

Warning

Draft The legal deadlines below are taken from Directive (EU) 2024/3019 [1]. The reference dates used in the reporting tables are draft design and may change.

After the first programme#

According to the draft, the Derogation table is reported in the first programme and afterwards only if derogation requests change. The Directive requires the programme itself to be updated at least every six years.
